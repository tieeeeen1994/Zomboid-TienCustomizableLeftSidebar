description=[list]
description=[*][b]Drag and drop:[/b] open the Customize button at the foot of the sidebar and drag the buttons up and down the list. The sidebar moves with you as you drag.
description=[*][b]Hide what you never use:[/b] click a button's eye to hide or show it. The buttons below close the gap.
description=[*][b]Spacing:[/b] from 0 to 40 pixels between buttons (the game's own is 15).
description=[*][b]Works with other mods:[/b] buttons other mods add to the sidebar are listed too, and new ones show up where their mod put them.
description=[*][b]The game still decides:[/b] buttons the game only shows sometimes (Admin, PVP safety, War) keep following its rules, in the place you gave them.
description=[*][b]Quick actions:[/b] right-click the sidebar to hide the button under the mouse, show hidden buttons again, or open the window.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=All in the Customize window: the order, which buttons show, the spacing, and the Customize button itself (full size, small, or hidden). Reset puts everything back the way the game has it.
description=Bind a key to [i]Customize the sidebar[/i] in Key Bindings (not bound by default) to open the window from the keyboard.
description=The layout is saved on your computer (Zomboid/Lua/TienCustomizableLeftSidebar.ini), so it follows you to every save and server.
